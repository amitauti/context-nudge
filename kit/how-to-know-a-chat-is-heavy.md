# How to Know a Chat Is Heavy

You do not need a token counter, developer tool, or browser extension to know when to restart. Watch for these everyday cues:

- **40+ messages:** You have exchanged roughly 40 or more back-and-forths, or you find yourself scrolling endlessly to find earlier points.
- **Generic answers:** Responses feel bland, vague, or lack the sharp, specific detail the AI gave earlier.
- **Repeating past mistakes:** The AI makes errors you already corrected 20 minutes ago, or forgets a character name, rule, or preference you set up earlier.
- **Browser lag:** Typing becomes sluggish, the browser tab consumes noticeable memory, or the AI takes noticeably longer to begin generating replies.
- **Natural milestone:** You just finished a major section (outlined a chapter, settled a budget, screened sources) and are about to start a new phase.

When you notice any one or two of these signs, don't struggle through the lag. Paste the [ledger prompt](ledger-prompt.md), copy the output, and start fresh with the [restart prompt](restart-prompt.md).
