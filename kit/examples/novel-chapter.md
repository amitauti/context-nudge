# Worked Example: Novel Chapter Writing

**Scenario:** A novelist working on Chapter 7 of a sci-fi mystery novel ("The Kepler Drift"). After 45 messages plotting the scene, exploring clues, and revising dialogue, the chat has become sluggish and the AI started forgetting secondary character names. The writer runs the ledger prompt, copies the output, and resumes in a fresh chat.

---

## 1. What the Old Chat Produces (The Ledger)

```markdown
## Date: 2026-11-22
## Goal / status
- Complete draft of Chapter 7 ("The Airlock Seal").
- Clue discovery scene outlined; opening 600 words written.

## Decisions
- Dr. Aris is the saboteur, but motivated by panic, not malice — keeps him sympathetic.
- Maya discovers the altered bypass cipher in the atmospheric log, not the maintenance bay — aligns with her communications role.
- Keep pace taut by setting the scene during an impending orbital decay alert — creates real-time tension.

## Constraints / preferences
- Third-person limited POV (Maya only).
- Grounded, gritty hard sci-fi tone; avoid melodramatic internal monologues.
- Elena remains unaware of Aris's involvement until Chapter 9.
- Target word count for chapter: 2,500 words.

## Documents / sources / files
- chapter-06-final.md — referenced — check final handoff scene at cargo deck.
- station-blueprint-draft.png — referenced — verify airlock 3 access corridor layout.
- chapter-07-draft.md — created — first 600 words containing opening dialogue.

## Open items / blockers
- How does Aris subtly explain his presence near Airlock 3 when Maya enters?
- Confirm whether the manual override requires two keys or a keycard.

## Next step
- Draft the confrontational dialogue between Maya and Aris inside Airlock 3 control booth.
```

*(Word count: 184 words — well within the 250-word cap)*

---

## 2. What Is Pasted into the Brand New Chat

```markdown
I am resuming an ongoing project from a previous session. Below is my handoff ledger containing the project state, settled decisions, constraints, and the next step.

Please follow these instructions:
1. Treat decisions as settled: Do NOT re-explore, question, second-guess, or alter decisions listed in the ledger unless I explicitly ask you to revisit them. Respect all stated constraints and preferences.
2. Staleness check: Check the date line at the top of the ledger. If the date is more than 2 days ago (or if the date is missing), flag this immediately: state that the handoff is older than 2 days, and ask if the context is still current before doing any work.
3. If the handoff is current (within 2 days):
   Do NOT generate drafts, solutions, or pages of text yet.
   Reply ONLY with:
   - Goal: (1 sentence confirming the current goal)
   - Next step: (1 sentence confirming the single immediate next action)
   - "Ready to proceed?"
4. Wait for my confirmation ("Yes", "Go ahead", or any adjustments) before starting the actual work.
```

## 3. How the New Chat Responds

```markdown
Goal: Complete the draft of Chapter 7 ("The Airlock Seal") in third-person limited POV focusing on Maya discovering the sabotage clue under orbital decay tension.

Next step: Draft the confrontational dialogue between Maya and Dr. Aris inside the Airlock 3 control booth.

Ready to proceed?
```

The author replies "Yes, let's start with Aris pretending to inspect the pressure seals when Maya enters", and writing continues immediately without re-explaining the plot or characters.
