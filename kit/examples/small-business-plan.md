# Worked Example: Small-Business Plan

**Scenario:** An entrepreneur planning the launch of "Crumb & Hearth", a neighborhood artisan sourdough bakery and espresso bar. After extensive sessions calculating equipment expenses, lease terms, wholesale flour costs, and staff scheduling, the chat thread has grown very long and the AI has started rounding numbers inaccurately. The owner captures the ledger and starts a fresh session.

---

## 1. What the Old Chat Produces (The Ledger)

```markdown
## Date: 2026-11-22
## Goal / status
- Complete 3-year financial pro-forma and operations plan for Crumb & Hearth bakery.
- Year 1 revenue model and equipment procurement schedule finalized; working on staff payroll schedule.

## Decisions
- Lease space at 442 Elm Street (1,200 sq ft, $3,200/month triple net) — prime morning commuter foot traffic.
- Purchase refurbished Bongard deck oven ($18,500) instead of new ($34,000) — frees $15,500 capital for initial operating reserves.
- Core opening product mix: 4 heritage sourdough loaves, 3 viennoiserie pastries, batch brew and espresso drinks — limits opening production complexity.
- Price signature country sourdough loaf at $9.50 — matches local premium benchmark and yields 78% gross margin.

## Constraints / preferences
- Total initial startup funding capped at $145,000 (owner equity + SBA microloan).
- Maintain minimum 3 months operating cash reserve ($28,000) untouched at launch.
- Target opening date: September 1, 2027.

## Documents / sources / files
- equipment-quote-revised.pdf — referenced — confirmed oven and spiral mixer pricing.
- pro-forma-v3.xlsx — touched — updated Year 1 COGS and fixed overhead figures.
- operations-plan.md — created — drafted morning baking shift timeline (3:00 AM - 11:00 AM).

## Open items / blockers
- Health department clearance for secondary wastewater grease trap installation.
- Finalize quote for commercial POS system (Square for Restaurants vs Toast).

## Next step
- Build Year 1 staffing matrix (head baker, apprentice, 2 part-time baristas) with hourly wages and payroll taxes.
```

*(Word count: 219 words — well within the 250-word cap)*

---

## 2. What Is Pasted into the Brand New Chat

```markdown
I am resuming an ongoing project from a previous session. Below is my handoff ledger containing the project state, settled decisions, constraints, and the next step.

Please follow these instructions:
1. Treat decisions as settled: Do NOT re-explore, question, second-guess, or alter decisions listed in the ledger unless I explicitly ask you to revisit them. Respect all stated constraints and preferences.
2. Staleness check: Check the date line at the top of the ledger. If the date is more than 2 days ago (or if the date is missing), flag this immediately: state that the handoff is older than 2 days, and ask if the context is still current before doing any work.
3. If the handoff is current (within 2 days):
   Do NOT generate drafts, solutions, or pages of text yet.
   Reply ONLY with:
   - Goal: (1 sentence confirming the current goal)
   - Next step: (1 sentence confirming the single immediate next action)
   - "Ready to proceed?"
4. Wait for my confirmation ("Yes", "Go ahead", or any adjustments) before starting the actual work.

---
```

## 3. How the New Chat Responds

```markdown
Goal: Complete the 3-year financial pro-forma and operations plan for Crumb & Hearth bakery within the $145,000 capital budget and September 2027 launch timeline.

Next step: Build the Year 1 staffing matrix (head baker, apprentice baker, and 2 part-time baristas) including wage rates, shift hours, and estimated payroll taxes.

Ready to proceed?
```

The business owner replies "Yes, assume $24/hr for the apprentice and $17/hr for baristas", and work moves forward without rehashing equipment or lease negotiations.
