# Worked Example: Academic Literature Review

**Scenario:** A graduate researcher synthesizing papers on "Urban heat island (UHI) mitigation via intensive green roofs in subtropical zones". After reviewing dozens of abstracts, comparing quantitative findings, and debating taxonomy over 50 messages, the chat is bloated. The researcher generates the ledger, clears the chat, and restarts cleanly.

---

## 1. What the Old Chat Produces (The Ledger)

```markdown
## Date: 2026-11-22
## Goal / status
- Synthesize empirical findings on intensive vs extensive green roofs for subtropical UHI mitigation.
- 28 studies screened; 14 selected for final quantitative matrix; draft of Section 3 (Thermal Performance) in progress.

## Decisions
- Group studies primarily by climatic sub-zone (Köppen Cfa vs Cwa) rather than roof plant species — climate zone isolates variance more reliably.
- Restrict quantitative comparison to surface temperature reduction (°C) and cooling energy savings (%) — indoor ambient temperature data was inconsistently reported.
- Exclude pre-2015 papers lacking microclimatic sensor calibration data — ensures data quality across comparative tables.

## Constraints / preferences
- Academic, formal tone adhering to APA 7th style guidelines.
- Emphasize empirical field measurements over purely computational ENVI-met simulations.
- Word count budget for Section 3: 1,500 words.

## Documents / sources / files
- literature-matrix.xlsx — touched — updated with 14 finalized studies and effect sizes.
- Santamouris_2020_Review.pdf — referenced — foundational benchmark for cooling metrics.
- section-3-draft.docx — created — initial outline and introductory paragraphs.

## Open items / blockers
- Reconcile contradictory cooling findings between Zhang et al. (2021) and Chen & Lin (2023) regarding substrate depth.
- Obtain full-text access for Oliveira (2024) through university interlibrary loan.

## Next step
- Draft subsection 3.2 comparing substrate depth influence (150mm vs 300mm) on peak daytime surface cooling.
```

*(Word count: 206 words — well within the 250-word cap)*

---

## 2. What Is Pasted into the Brand New Chat

```markdown

Please follow these instructions:
1. Treat decisions as settled: Do NOT re-explore, question, second-guess, or alter decisions listed in the ledger unless I explicitly ask you to revisit them. Respect all stated constraints and preferences.
2. Staleness check: Check the date line at the top of the ledger. If the date is more than 2 days ago (or if the date is missing), flag this immediately: state that the handoff is older than 2 days, and ask if the context is still current before doing any work.
3. If the handoff is current (within 2 days):
   Do NOT generate drafts, solutions, or pages of text yet.
   Reply ONLY with:
   - Goal: (1 sentence confirming the current goal)
   - Next step: (1 sentence confirming the single immediate next action)
   - "Ready to proceed?"
4. Wait for my confirmation ("Yes", "Go ahead", or any adjustments) before starting the actual work.
```
